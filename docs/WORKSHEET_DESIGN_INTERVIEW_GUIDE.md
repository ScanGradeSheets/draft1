# Worksheet Design Interview Guide

Created 2026-05-30 for a future Tony/Codex worksheet-quality interview.

This guide turns the unresolved worksheet taste questions into a structured conversation. It should be used when Tony is ready, not as an interruption.

## Goal

Help Codex understand what makes a ScanGrade worksheet good enough for Tony to:

- use in class
- put the ScanGrade name on
- sell on TPT
- scale into a product family

## How To Use

Use this as a conversational guide, not a survey.

Start with Tony's instincts, then ask follow-ups only where the answer reveals a design rule.

## Part 1: Instant Reactions

Ask Tony:

1. What makes you instantly trust a worksheet?
2. What makes you instantly reject a worksheet?
3. What feels "teacher-made in a good way" versus "generic TPT filler"?
4. What feels too childish for Grade 2?
5. What feels too sterile or test-like?

Useful follow-ups:

- Is the problem visual, instructional, or just taste?
- Would this matter to students, teachers, buyers, or all three?

## Part 2: White Space And Density

Ask Tony:

1. How much white space feels right for Grade 1/2?
2. When does a page feel too empty?
3. When does a page feel crowded?
4. How many questions per page feels right for practice?
5. How many questions per page feels right for quick checks or assessment?

Current default:

- 10 questions per page for the active Grade 2 scan format.

Decision needed:

- Is 10 questions the ScanGrade default, or only the current test format?

## Part 3: Answer Boxes

Ask Tony:

1. Do the current open-divider boxes feel intuitive to students?
2. Do they look too technical?
3. Are they big enough for normal Grade 1/2 handwriting?
4. Should answer boxes feel like writing space, digit slots, or something in between?
5. What should happen for one-digit answers in a two-digit box?

Decision needed:

- Whether the open-divider answer box becomes the canonical ScanGrade style.

## Part 4: Typography

Ask Tony:

1. Does Lexend feel right for student worksheets?
2. Should titles feel more playful, more formal, or stay simple?
3. What fonts make you reject a worksheet instantly?
4. Should student pages and teacher/product pages use different type hierarchy?

Current default:

- Lexend for worksheet text and product consistency.

## Part 5: Decoration And Fun

Ask Tony:

1. How much fun belongs on a ScanGrade worksheet?
2. Are icons or illustrations ever helpful?
3. Should early elementary sheets have any playful visual cues?
4. What kind of decoration feels like filler?
5. Should seasonal packs exist later, or does that dilute the brand?

Current default:

- No decorative filler on scan-critical student pages.
- Product covers/previews may carry more visual personality.

## Part 6: Color

Ask Tony:

1. Should student worksheets stay black and white?
2. Is subtle gray helpful or distracting?
3. Should ScanGrade green appear on student pages at all?
4. Should TPT previews and covers use more color than the worksheets?

Current default:

- Student pages mostly black/white/gray.
- ScanGrade green reserved for app, covers, previews, and action/success states.

## Part 7: Brand Presence

Ask Tony:

1. How visible should `ScanGrade.io` be on student pages?
2. Where should the URL live?
3. Should the logo appear on every worksheet?
4. What would feel too promotional for a student page?
5. What would be too hidden for photocopier discovery?

Current default:

- Keep `ScanGrade.io` visible but quiet.

## Part 8: Product Families

Ask Tony:

1. Should ScanGrade products feel like a consistent system?
2. How much variety should exist between packs?
3. What should make a math fact pack, intervention pack, and assessment pack visually different?
4. Should Grade 1 look meaningfully different from Grade 2?
5. What must stay consistent across all ScanGrade worksheets?

## Part 9: TPT Buyer Lens

Ask Tony:

1. What makes you believe a TPT worksheet is worth buying?
2. What makes a preview convincing?
3. What product claims feel honest?
4. What claims feel like hype?
5. Would you prefer smaller premium packs or larger value bundles?

## Part 10: Final Decisions To Capture

The interview should produce decisions on:

- default question count by format
- answer-box style
- color rules
- illustration rules
- title/header style
- `ScanGrade.io` placement
- worksheet density
- first paid product size
- what Tony rejects instantly
- what "premium but practical" means in examples

## Output After Interview

Codex should turn Tony's answers into:

1. updates to `docs/WORKSHEET_DESIGN_SYSTEM.md`
2. updates to `docs/WORKSHEET_QUALITY_RUBRIC.md`
3. a small set of worksheet visual directions
4. TPT preview/cover rules
