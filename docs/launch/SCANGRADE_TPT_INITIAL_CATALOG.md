# ScanGrade Initial TPT Catalog

Status: founder-approved worksheet-first strategy; launch copy remains gated on prospective classroom validation.

## Catalog rule

Launch as a small, coherent Grade 1 math worksheet brand. Every product must be useful as an ordinary printable even if a teacher never scans it. Auto-grading at `scangrade.io` is a free companion benefit, not a separately billed service.

Do not publish a listing until:

- every included QR code resolves to the correct frozen worksheet layout;
- the printable and answer key have been visually checked;
- the current public grader completes every page on the supported devices;
- teacher review can correct every yellow answer;
- the listing uses the approved reliability language;
- the product has passed the September prospective validation gate, or is explicitly labelled a limited beta.

## Listing structure

Suggested title:

> Grade 1 Math Worksheets With Free Scan-and-Grade Companion | 10-Page Beta Sampler

Suggested first sentence:

> Print familiar Grade 1 math practice, then use the free ScanGrade website to mark clear answers and quickly review handwriting the system is unsure about.

Required listing sections:

- What is included
- Skills covered
- How scanning works
- What happens when handwriting is unclear
- Device and printing requirements
- Privacy and storage statement
- Beta limitations
- Terms of use

## Pricing rule

At listing time, compare current TPT prices for similarly sized, high-quality Grade 1 math packs and price within that range. Do not charge a separate software fee. Do not set a permanent price from this document because marketplace pricing is time-sensitive and requires founder approval.

## Expansion rule

Only scale a worksheet family after:

- at least 20 prospective classroom pages for that family;
- zero known catastrophic confident transcription errors;
- a measured median teacher-review time that beats manual marking;
- low support burden in the beta;
- buyers demonstrate repeat interest.
