## Page 3 - Familiar paper, faster feedback

1. Print the worksheet.
2. Students complete it in pencil.
3. Scan the whole page at `scangrade.io`.
4. Review any answer highlighted in yellow.
5. Check the final marked page.

## Page 4 - Teacher review stays in control

Yellow means "please confirm the handwriting," not "the math is wrong."

Tap a yellow answer, choose a suggestion, type what the student wrote, or save it as blank. ScanGrade does not silently replace uncertain handwriting with the correct mathematical answer.

## Page 5 - Honest beta expectations

ScanGrade marks clear answers and asks for help with uncertain handwriting. Results can vary with handwriting, lighting, page angle, erasures, and device quality. Review the marked page before using it for feedback or records.

The beta supports the included ScanGrade-authored worksheets. It does not promise to grade arbitrary worksheets.

## Page 6 - Try the classroom beta

The worksheet is useful with or without the website.

What you need:

- A printer
- A phone or tablet with a camera
- Internet access to `scangrade.io`
- A teacher checking the final result

Suggested use: independent practice, homework review, centers, intervention, or formative feedback.
