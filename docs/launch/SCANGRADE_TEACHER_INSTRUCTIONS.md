# ScanGrade Teacher Instructions

## Print, complete, scan, review

1. Print the worksheet at actual size. Do not crop away the black corner squares or QR code.
2. Students write one answer in each answer area using pencil or dark pen.
3. Open `https://scangrade.io` on a phone or tablet. No account is required for the beta.
4. Tap **Start Scan**, hold the whole page in view, and capture it in even light.
5. ScanGrade marks the answers it can read and highlights uncertain handwriting in yellow.
6. Tap a yellow answer. Choose the matching suggestion or type what the student actually wrote.
7. Check the final marked page before using the result for feedback or records.

## The important rule

Confirm what the student wrote, not what the mathematically correct answer should have been. A confidently transcribed wrong math answer should receive a red X. An unclear handwritten answer should stay yellow until a teacher confirms it.

## For the best scan

- Put the worksheet on a flat, contrasting surface.
- Keep all four black corner squares visible.
- Avoid a hand, phone shadow, or strong glare over answer boxes.
- Hold the device approximately parallel to the page.
- If the page is blurred, badly angled, cut off, or incorrectly marked, scan it again.

## Yellow review

Yellow does not mean the math is wrong. It means ScanGrade wants the teacher to confirm the handwriting. The review box starts empty so the teacher can enter the observed answer without being forced to accept the computer's guess.

- Tap a suggestion to apply it immediately.
- Type a different digit when none of the suggestions match.
- Leave the field empty and tap **Save** to record a blank.
- In a two-box answer area, ScanGrade preserves which physical box contains the teacher-confirmed digit.

## What ScanGrade supports in the beta

- The included ScanGrade-authored Grade 1 worksheets
- Phones and tablets with a current browser
- A five-year-old iPad-class screen and browser layout
- Handwritten numerical answers in the printed answer areas

## What the beta does not promise

- Arbitrary third-party worksheets
- Perfect recognition of every handwriting style
- Automatic resolution of overwritten, crossed-out, reversed, faint, or clipped digits
- A replacement for teacher judgment
- A permanent student record or gradebook

## Privacy

The public beta is designed to grade in the browser without requiring an account. Teachers should still follow school and board policies for student work. Do not include unnecessary identifying information in test scans. The published privacy statement must describe any operational logs or temporary processing accurately before paid launch.

## If something looks wrong

Do not accept the mark silently. Correct the highlighted answer, rescan the page, or mark the page manually. Report the worksheet code, device type, and what happened without publicly sharing identifiable student work.
