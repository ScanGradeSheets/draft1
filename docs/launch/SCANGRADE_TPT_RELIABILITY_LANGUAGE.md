# ScanGrade Reliability Language

Status: approved draft language for a limited beta. Numerical claims remain prohibited until prospective validation.

## Safe short promise

> ScanGrade automatically marks clear handwritten answers and highlights uncertain ones for quick teacher review.

## Safe fuller description

> These worksheets are designed to work with the free ScanGrade companion website. Scan a completed page with a phone or tablet. ScanGrade marks answers it can read and highlights handwriting that needs teacher confirmation. Teachers remain in control of the final result.

## Required beta disclosure

> ScanGrade is in beta. Handwriting, lighting, page angle, erasures, and device quality can affect results. Review the marked page before using it for feedback or records. If a scan is unclear or incorrectly marked, correct it in the review tool, rescan it, or grade it manually.

## Safe differentiation

- Purpose-built printable layouts with a free scan-and-grade companion
- No student device is required while completing the worksheet
- Uncertain handwriting is surfaced for teacher review
- Teacher corrections are based on the observed handwriting
- The worksheets remain usable without the website

## Claims not supported

Do not publish any of the following:

- "90% accurate"
- "100% accurate"
- "error-free"
- "grades every page automatically"
- "works with any worksheet"
- "eliminates marking"
- "AI-powered grading you can trust without checking"
- "saves X minutes per class" until measured prospectively
- "student data is never stored" until the full public request and logging path is independently verified

## Evidence language for internal use

The current 50-sheet, five-packet retrospective public-version audit contains 345 scorable hand-labelled answers. Beta 15.3/Beta 7 automatically accepted 273 answers (79.1% coverage), but 20 accepted transcriptions were wrong. All 20 came from one particularly difficult packet; the other four packets had zero observed confident errors. This is not launch-grade evidence and must not be converted into a public accuracy claim.

The interface itself passed current-phone and five-year-old-iPad-sized saved-sheet tests for blank handling, correction placement, overlay layering, annotation containment, and fixed-screen layout. Those are engineering gates, not claims about classroom outcomes.

September classroom work is the decisive prospective evidence. Retrospective tuning data must not be presented as independent launch validation.

## Prospective claim gate

A numerical public claim requires:

- a preregistered sample and frozen production build;
- unseen students and packets;
- every transcription labelled from handwriting, not the answer key;
- confidence intervals;
- separate automatic coverage and accuracy-among-automatic metrics;
- disclosure of teacher-review rate and capture failures;
- zero known catastrophic confident transcription errors;
- founder approval of the exact wording.
