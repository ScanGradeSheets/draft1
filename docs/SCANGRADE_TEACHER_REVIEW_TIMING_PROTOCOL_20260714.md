# Six-answer teacher review timing protocol

Use P08 page 1, P03 page 2, and P02 page 4. These pages are already part of development/evaluation; do not open another packet.

For each page:

1. Scan normally.
2. When the marked result appears, start reviewing immediately—do not wait deliberately for AI suggestions.
3. Tap the first yellow answer and choose what the child actually wrote.
4. Continue until no yellow answers remain. Do not change confidently wrong red answers.
5. Record overall page-review time, any missing choice/manual entry, any red answer opened automatically, and confidence from 1–5.

Expected yellow counts are 3, 1, and 2. Stop if a red answer opens automatically or a correction changes another question.

Pass gate: 6/6 final transcriptions correct, no red auto-open, no keypad needed, median answer decision at most 3 seconds, no decision above 6 seconds, and confidence at least 4/5.
