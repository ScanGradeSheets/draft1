# ScanGrade Marketing Asset Pipeline

Created 2026-05-30 as internal planning for future teacher-facing promotion.

This is not a launch plan by itself. It defines what assets to prepare once the worksheet format and classroom scan loop have enough evidence.

## Purpose

ScanGrade marketing should demonstrate the real paper workflow:

1. Print.
2. Students complete.
3. Scan.
4. Review.

The marketing should not exaggerate. It should show teachers what the product does, why it saves time, and where teacher judgment remains in control.

## Asset Principles

Marketing assets should be:

- honest
- teacher-facing
- clean
- concrete
- based on real worksheets and screenshots
- conservative about scan/grading claims

They should not be:

- generic edtech hype
- heavy on AI language
- full of fake classroom promises
- vague lifestyle imagery
- disconnected from the actual app

## Asset Tiers

### Tier 1: Safe Before Classroom Scan Proof

Allowed now:

- worksheet preview images
- answer-box close-ups
- product cover/thumbnail concepts
- TPT copy drafts
- teacher use notes
- static "paper-first workflow" diagrams without scan guarantees
- Mission Control/internal planning assets

Avoid:

- app success screenshots implying proven automation
- "instant grading" language
- claims that ScanGrade saves a specific amount of time

### Tier 2: After Real Student Sample Test

Allowed if evidence supports it:

- before/after teacher review examples
- real scan result screenshots with caveats
- failure/review flag examples
- "designed for ScanGrade review" language
- classroom test summary for internal use

Still avoid:

- broad generalization beyond tested sheets
- guaranteed accuracy
- replacing teacher judgment

### Tier 3: After Small Classroom Pilot

Allowed if Tony approves:

- short demo workflow
- landing page screenshots
- stronger ScanGrade-compatible product language
- teacher testimonial or classroom quote if real and approved
- simple launch posts

Still avoid:

- unsupported achievement claims
- "no marking ever"
- "works with every worksheet"

## Core Asset Set For First TPT Product

### Product Cover

Purpose:

- make the product understandable at thumbnail size

Should include:

- grade level
- skill
- "No Prep Practice"
- worksheet count
- answer key included
- small ScanGrade.io branding

Should avoid:

- clutter
- tiny text
- heavy decorative theme

### Preview PDF / Images

Should include:

- full worksheet page
- answer-box close-up
- 3-page or 12-page overview
- answer key preview
- teacher use note

Possible captions:

- "Large answer boxes for student writing"
- "Clean layout for quick checking"
- "Answer key included"
- "Works as regular printable practice"

### Teacher Use Note

One page or section:

- when to use it
- what is included
- how to print
- how the answer key works
- ScanGrade note only if approved

### ScanGrade Note

Before scan proof:

> These pages are designed with the ScanGrade paper-first review workflow in mind, but they work as regular printable worksheets too.

After scan proof:

> These pages are designed to work with ScanGrade's scanning and review workflow, helping paper practice move more quickly into teacher review.

## Launch Channel Ideas

### TPT

Primary channel for worksheet products.

Needs:

- clear title
- clean cover
- preview PDF/images
- accurate included-files list
- conservative promise

### ScanGrade.io

Eventually:

- simple landing page
- teacher workflow demo
- worksheet sample download
- explanation of paper-first scanning
- waitlist or contact route

### Social / Teacher Posts

Possible post themes:

- "Paper practice, faster review"
- "Large answer boxes matter"
- "What makes a worksheet scan-friendly?"
- "Print, practice, review"
- "Built for real Grade 1/2 handwriting"

Do not post claims before classroom proof.

## Screenshot Rules

Use real app screenshots only.

Screenshots should show:

- simple student scan flow
- clear success/retry states
- teacher review queue
- review flags
- teacher override/control

Avoid screenshots with:

- developer controls
- OCR/model jargon
- fake results
- over-polished states not in the actual app

## Relationship To Current Mission

The current mission is still the classroom sample test.

Marketing preparation is useful while waiting, but classroom evidence controls what can be published.
