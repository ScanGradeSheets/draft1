# Grade 2 First Paid Pack Blueprint

Created 2026-05-30 from ScanGrade's launch framework and a small TPT market scan.

This is an internal product blueprint. It should not move into production until the current open-divider worksheet format is validated with real student samples.

## Why This Exists

The current 3-sheet open-divider packet is the right size for classroom validation.

It is probably not enough to be ScanGrade's main first paid TPT product unless used as:

- a free sample
- a low-price sampler
- a launch preview
- a classroom-tested mini pack

A stronger first paid pack should likely contain 10-20 polished student pages, while preserving one proven format.

## Recommended Product Shape

Working title:

> Grade 2 Addition and Subtraction Worksheets - No Prep Practice Pack

Possible subtitle:

> Clean math practice pages with large answer boxes and answer keys included

Recommended size:

- 12 student pages
- 1 combined packet PDF
- 1 teacher answer key PDF
- 1 preview PDF/image set
- 1 terms-of-use page
- 1 short teacher use note

Why 12 pages:

- large enough to feel like a real product
- small enough to keep quality high
- easy to derive from the current 3-sheet family after validation
- avoids jumping straight to a broad bundle before the format is proven

## Page Rules

Until classroom validation says otherwise:

- keep 10 questions per page
- keep the open-divider answer boxes
- keep the two-column structure
- keep corner markers and QR/layout system unchanged
- keep `ScanGrade.io` visible but quiet
- keep student pages mostly black/white/gray
- do not add decorative themes

## Teacher Use Note

The product should include a short plain-language page:

```text
Use these pages for morning work, math centers, review, homework, or quick checks.

Students write answers in the large boxes. An answer key is included for quick review.

These pages are designed with the ScanGrade workflow in mind, but they work as regular printable worksheets too.
```

Before ScanGrade classroom evidence, avoid saying the sheets are automatically graded.

## Recommendation

Use the current 3-sheet packet as the validation set and possible sampler.

If it performs well, build the 12-page paid pack next. That gives ScanGrade a real marketplace product without pretending the whole worksheet ecosystem exists yet.
