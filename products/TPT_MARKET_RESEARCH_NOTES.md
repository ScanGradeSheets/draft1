## Common Marketplace Patterns

### Title Patterns

Common title ingredients:

- grade level
- operation or skill
- regrouping/no regrouping
- no prep
- worksheets
- bundle or pack
- answer key
- sometimes centers, games, exit tickets, assessments, or review

Implication for ScanGrade:

- Lead with ordinary teacher search language first.
- Do not make `ScanGrade-compatible` the first phrase until the app promise is proven and meaningful.

Good working shape:

> Grade 2 Addition and Subtraction Worksheets - No Prep Math Practice Mini Pack

### Offer Size

The market has both small packs and very large bundles:

- small mixed packs around 15 pages
- mid-size sets around 44-60 worksheet pages
- large bundles around 200+ or 270 worksheet pages
- answer keys are commonly included and expected

Implication for ScanGrade:

- The current 3-sheet packet is probably too small to be the main paid product unless positioned as a sampler, pilot mini pack, or free/low-price lead magnet.
- A stronger first paid pack may need 10-20 student pages after the format is validated.
- Do not expand quantity before the open-divider format works with real student handwriting.

### Use Cases Buyers Recognize

Common use cases:

- homework
- math centers
- morning work
- review
- independent work
- early finishers
- intervention or RTI
- sub plans
- quick checks / exit tickets
- summer review

Implication for ScanGrade:

- ScanGrade copy should attach to familiar routines, not introduce a new routine first.
- Best early language: "math centers, morning work, review, homework, and quick checks."

### Feature Claims

Common claims:

- no prep
- print and go
- clear layouts
- answer keys included
- skill practice
- standards alignment
- with/without regrouping
- visual supports such as base ten blocks
- games, task cards, and exit tickets in broader packs

Implication for ScanGrade:

- Safe early claims overlap with marketplace expectations: no-prep, clean, easy to review, answer key included.
- ScanGrade's eventual differentiator should be workflow: paper practice that can flow into quicker review.
- Do not claim fully automated grading until the app proves it in Tony's classroom.

## Differentiation Opportunity

Most comparable products compete on quantity, theme, breadth, or standards coverage.

ScanGrade can eventually compete on:

- paper-first workflow
- clean answer regions designed for quick review
- compatibility with a scanning/review loop
- teacher trust and review flags
- premium, uncluttered layout
- a repeatable worksheet family rather than one-off decorative pages

The strongest angle is not "more worksheets."

The strongest angle is:

> These are practical paper worksheets built for a faster review workflow.

## Risks From The Market Scan

### Risk: 3 Pages May Feel Too Small

The first current packet is excellent for validation but may feel thin as a paid TPT product.

Possible use:

- free sample
- low-price mini pack
- launch preview
- classroom-tested pilot pack

### Risk: Large Bundles Set A Value Expectation

Some competitors offer dozens or hundreds of pages.

Response:

- Do not compete on page count first.
- Compete on quality, clarity, classroom usability, and eventually ScanGrade workflow.
- Once format is proven, build a repeatable family so page count grows without reinventing layout.

### Risk: "ScanGrade-Compatible" Needs Explanation

TPT buyers may not know ScanGrade yet.

Response:

- Treat ScanGrade compatibility as a secondary note until there is a simple public explanation.
- Primary buyer promise should still make sense without the app.

### Risk: Overclaiming Would Damage Trust

The market uses strong convenience language, but ScanGrade should not imply unproven automation.

Response:

- Keep "designed for quick review" as the safe line.
- Move to "works with ScanGrade scanning" only after classroom evidence.

## Recommended First Product Strategy

### Before Student Samples

Prepare only:

- internal launch framework
- conservative listing draft
- preview concepts
- product sequence
- market notes

Do not publish.

### After Student Samples Pass Usability But Before Full App Proof

Possible product:

- free or low-price 3-sheet mini pack
- plain worksheet promise
- mention ScanGrade only as "designed with ScanGrade's review workflow in mind" if Tony approves

### After Scan Workflow Shows Teacher Value

Possible product:

- 10-20 page Grade 2 addition/subtraction pack
- answer key
- preview images
- quick-start note
- ScanGrade.io visible on sheets
- careful compatibility statement

## Copy Guidance

Safer phrases:

- no-prep Grade 2 math practice
- large answer boxes for student writing
- clean layouts for quick review
- answer key included
- designed for morning work, centers, review, homework, or quick checks
- built around a consistent ScanGrade-friendly format

Avoid until proven:

- auto-grades student work
- no marking required
- instant accurate results
- AI-powered assessment
- eliminates grading
- guaranteed to scan every worksheet
