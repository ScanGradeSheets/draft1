## What Must Be True Before Publishing

Do not publish until:

- Tony has collected real student-completed samples.
- The app has processed those samples with an acceptable review workflow.
- The answer boxes are confirmed to work for normal Grade 2 handwriting.
- Any required sheet geometry or OCR changes are made and re-tested.
- Tony approves the teacher-facing promise.

## First Listing Direction

Possible title:

- Grade 2 Addition and Subtraction Worksheets - No Prep Math Practice Mini Pack

Possible subtitle:

- Large answer boxes, clean layout, quick review format

Avoid for now:

- "Auto-grading"
- "Instantly scans every sheet"
- "AI-powered"
- "No marking ever"

Safer copy:

- Designed for quick classroom practice and easy teacher review.
- Simple layouts reduce visual clutter.
- Large answer boxes give students room to write clearly.
- Works as printable practice now; ScanGrade scanning support is being classroom-tested.

## Buyer Promise

The first product should promise:

- Low-prep math practice
- Clear student writing space
- Repeatable worksheet format
- Easy teacher review

It should not yet promise:

- Perfect scanning
- Fully automated marking
- Multi-class analytics
- Gradebook replacement
