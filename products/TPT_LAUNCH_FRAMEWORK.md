# ScanGrade TPT Launch Framework

Created 2026-05-30 as internal launch planning. This is not a public promise and should not be copied directly into a marketplace listing.

## Purpose

This framework turns ScanGrade's long-term marketplace goal into a cautious sequence of proof points and artifacts.

The aim is to reach a small, trustworthy TPT launch without getting ahead of classroom evidence.

## North Star

Start with a small set of excellent paper worksheets that work even without the app.

Then, once the classroom scan loop is proven, let ScanGrade compatibility become the reason the product line is different.

## Launch Principle

Do not launch a broad worksheet store first.

Launch a narrow, polished, repeatable product family:

- one grade band
- one worksheet format
- one visual language
- one answer-box style
- one teacher promise
- one validation story

The first product should feel boringly reliable before it feels ambitious.

## Readiness Gates

### Gate 1: Worksheet Usability

Evidence needed:

- Students know where to write.
- Students do not consistently fight the answer boxes.
- The page feels clean and classroom-normal.
- Tony would actually hand it to students again.

Marketplace implication:

- Safe to describe the sheets as clear, no-prep, and easy to review.

### Gate 2: Scan Compatibility

Evidence needed:

- Real student-completed sheets run through the current app.
- QR/layout/crops work on normal photos.
- Failure modes are understandable.
- Uncertain answers are flagged rather than guessed confidently.

Marketplace implication:

- Safe to mention that the format is ScanGrade-compatible or designed for ScanGrade testing, depending on evidence.

### Gate 3: Teacher Trust

Evidence needed:

- Review feels faster than marking from scratch.
- Tony can explain when the app is right, uncertain, or wrong.
- Corrections/overrides feel teacher-owned.

Marketplace implication:

- Safe to start talking about saved marking time, while avoiding absolute automation claims.

### Gate 4: Product Polish

Evidence needed:

- Final PDFs are clean.
- Answer key is easy to skim.
- Preview images are honest and attractive.
- Terms of use are included.
- Listing copy is conservative and clear.

Marketplace implication:

- Ready for Tony review before publishing.

## First Product Promise

Safe promise before broad validation:

> Clean no-prep Grade 2 practice pages with large answer boxes, an included answer key, and a layout designed for quick teacher review.

Possible ScanGrade promise after classroom evidence:

> Designed to work with the ScanGrade scanning workflow, so paper practice can move more quickly into teacher review.

Avoid:

- fully automated grading
- no marking ever
- guaranteed recognition
- AI-powered learning gains
- replaces assessment
- perfect scanning

## Product Family Rules

Each product should have:

- a student PDF
- a combined print packet
- a teacher answer key
- a preview image/PDF
- terms of use
- a short usage note
- a clear skill focus
- visible but quiet `ScanGrade.io` branding

Each product should avoid:

- new scan-critical geometry unless tested
- decorative filler
- broad curriculum claims
- app claims that exceed evidence
- too many variants before one format is trusted

## TPT Listing Voice

Tone:

- teacher-to-teacher
- practical
- calm
- honest
- lightly warm

Good copy patterns:

- "Use for morning work, math centers, review, or homework."
- "Large answer boxes give students room to write."
- "Clean layout keeps the focus on the math."
- "Answer key included for quick checking."

Avoid copy patterns:

- "Transform your classroom."
- "Never mark again."
- "AI-powered achievement gains."
- "Revolutionary automated assessment."

## Preview Asset Rules

Preview assets should show the actual product clearly:

- full-page worksheet
- answer-box close-up
- included answer key
- packet overview
- honest classroom use case

Do not obscure the worksheet with heavy mockups, fake device screens, or vague lifestyle graphics.

## Market Research Checklist

Before final listing copy, do a small market scan and summarize in internal notes:

- common title patterns for Grade 2 math worksheet packs
- common preview structures
- typical bundle sizes
- common teacher pain points
- price/format signals if visible
- how competitors describe no-prep, answer keys, centers, homework, and review

Use research to understand expectations, not to copy language.

## Relationship To App Work

TPT work should not distract from the current app mission.

If real student samples reveal answer-box or scan problems, pause launch expansion and fix the classroom loop first.

The marketplace becomes valuable because the worksheets are both good paper products and part of a trustworthy ScanGrade workflow.
